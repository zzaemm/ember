The problem, in plain words

Satellites have recorded fires for 25 years

That history could show when each place usually burns.

A sharper satellite arrived in 2012

Like swapping a blurry camera for a sharp one, it counts far more small fires.

So the years can’t be compared

Nobody can tell a worse fire year from a better camera.

Ember puts both records on one scale, then turns them into a fire calendar anyone can read.

SPEAKER 1 (about 30 seconds). Here is the problem in plain words. Satellites have recorded fires for 25 years, and that history could tell every district when it usually burns. But in 2012 a sharper satellite arrived. Think of swapping a blurry camera for a sharp one: you suddenly count many more small fires. So when the numbers jump, nobody can tell a worse fire year from a better camera. Ember puts both records on one scale, then turns them into a calendar that anyone can read.
