QuestionAnswer
How do you know pre-2012 is right?We cannot test it directly. We show skill in the overlap, a stable ratio, and agreement with MODIS alone.
Does low Punjab mean less burning?Not necessarily. Late burning can escape both sensors, and our correction cannot see that.
Why not one global factor?It misses totals by +61% in the Northeast and −37% in Odisha. Ours gives +5% and −3%.
Doesn’t an officer already know the season?From experience, yes. We add 25 years of week-level evidence for planning and budgets.
What about MODIS orbit drift?Corrected MODIS stays at 1.02–1.04 of VIIRS across 2020–2025. After 2012 we use VIIRS anyway.
Why no backend?The data is read-only and updated in batches. Static files are cheap and resilient. The cost is 27 km resolution.
