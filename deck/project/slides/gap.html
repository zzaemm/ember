SPEAKER 1 (about 30 seconds). Here is that problem as one number. The older instrument, MODIS, has flown since 2000. The sharper one, VIIRS, joined in 2012. If you join them, India appears to become 5.7 times busier overnight. MODIS alone, across exactly the same years, shows 1.02 times. So a fire manager reading the raw record would see a crisis that the instrument created. The calendar we were asked to build cannot exist until that step is removed.
