SPEAKER 3 (about 35 seconds). We want to show you the weak spots ourselves. At the scale of India, the Northeast or central India, weekly error is 13 to 16 percent. In Kerala it is 47 percent, because Kerala has fewer and smaller fires. For a small area such as Wayanad it is over 100 percent, so week-by-week values before 2012 cannot be relied on there. The useful finding is that timing is much sturdier than totals. The busiest week usually matches exactly. The calendar answers when, and that is the question our users ask.
