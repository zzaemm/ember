This answers one question: was this week normal? Every week is compared with the same time of year in all other years. The week of 30 April 2024 had 4,914 detections, where 9 of 10 other years stayed below 3,032. That matches the severe fire season reported in the news that spring. Here is what it does not do. A roughly 10-acre fire was reported in Wayanad in February 2025, and that week reads as normal. This calendar works at the scale of seasons and regions, and is not an incident detector. Knowing that keeps people from trusting it for the wrong job.
