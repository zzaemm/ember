What Ember is, and what it is not

Live tools answer

“Where is it burning now?”

NASA FIRMS and Forest Survey of India alerts. We link to them and send no alerts.

Ember answers

“Is this normal here, and when should we be ready?”

It uses past patterns, and it is not a forecast.

Limits we show in the app

Satellites pass only at set times and miss small fires and fires under cloud, thick smoke or canopy. No detection does not mean no fire. Before 2012 cannot be tested directly. In 56% of active cell-months MODIS saw nothing while VIIRS did.

SPEAKER 5 (about 30 seconds). We do not compete with FIRMS or the Forest Survey of India. They answer where it is burning now, and we link to them. We answer whether this is normal here and when to be ready. We are also clear about limits, in the app itself and not only in a footnote. Satellites pass at set times and miss small fires and fires under smoke or canopy. Before 2012 cannot be tested directly. And in 56 percent of active grid-cell months, MODIS saw nothing while VIIRS did. Those months hold about 10 percent of VIIRS detections. For a tool people may act on, stating these limits is part of the product.
