SPEAKER 4 (about 30 seconds). Numbers alone do not tell you why. Every place in Ember now explains its usual cause: stubble burning in Punjab, jhum farming in the Northeast, dry undergrowth in Kerala. Tap a year and you see what was reported. Odisha in 2021 stands out: satellites saw about 4.1 times a usual year in those weeks, when the Similipal fire burned for over 10 days. Every story links to the original report, and the app compares it with the satellite record. When a serious local fire barely changes a wide region’s total, as in Mizoram in 2021, the app says so. These are stories we chose and checked, not a live feed, and a news link only shows what was reported at the time.
