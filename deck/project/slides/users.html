Who needs this calendar

Forest officer

Decides when to finish fire lines and hire watchers.

“Which weeks burn in my area, and is that moving earlier?”

Air quality analyst

Decides where to send stubble-burning field checks.

“Did the peak move, and is the drop in detections real?”

Researcher

Decides whether a long-term trend can be published.

“Does this trend survive on a single sensor?”

Working assumptions from public reporting. We have not yet checked these with officials.

SPEAKER 1 (about 30 seconds). We designed for three people. A forest officer deciding when fire lines must be finished and watchers hired. An air quality analyst deciding where to send field checks during stubble burning. A researcher deciding whether a trend is real. None of them need another map of today’s fires, because NASA FIRMS and the Forest Survey of India already do that. They need to know what is normal here and when to be ready. Be open that these personas are our assumptions; we have not yet interviewed an officer.
