SPEAKER 2 (about 45 seconds). The two sensors are measuring the same fires differently. VIIRS has 375 metre pixels, and MODIS has 1 kilometre pixels. In India, VIIRS finds 5.6 times as many hotspots but only 1.3 times as much total fire power. That gap is the signature of small fires that MODIS misses. So hotspot counts and fire power each need their own correction. The ratio also changes with place and fire type. Apply one national number and you overstate the Northeast by 61 percent and understate Odisha by 37 percent. The correction has to be local.
