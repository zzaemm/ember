SPEAKER 4 (about 35 seconds). Switch to Punjab and Haryana. The chart flips: a smaller peak around the wheat harvest in April and May, and a larger one around the paddy harvest in October and November. Three weeks, 22 October to 11 November, hold half of a typical year. Now the part that matters. Detections from late September to early December fell from about 85,000 in 2016 to about 7,000 in 2025. A naive dashboard would call that a 92 percent success. But an ISRO-linked study reported that the peak burning time moved from about 1:30 pm to about 5 pm, after the satellites pass, though Punjab officials dispute this. So we say detections fell, and we do not say burning fell by that much.
