# Ember: who presents what

The full word-for-word script is in the speaker notes of each slide. Times total about 9 minutes. If your slot is shorter, see "Cutting time" below.

| Speaker | Role | Slides | Time | The one thing they must land |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Problem and users | 1–6 | 3 min | A sharper satellite made the record look 5.7× busier, so nobody can compare the years |
| 2 | Earth science | 7–8 | 1.5 min | VIIRS finds 5.6× the hotspots but 1.3× the fire power, so the correction must be local |
| 3 | Validation | 9–10 | 1.5 min | Tested on unseen years: totals within 3%, and we state where it is weak |
| 4 | Live demo | 11–15 | 3 min | Anyone can read the answer and see why it burned, and experts can open the full record |
| 5 | Engineering and close | 16–18 | 1.5 min | No server, honest limits, and clear next steps |

Speaker 4 has the most time and risk, so choose your most confident presenter.

## Prepare individually

- **Speaker 1:** Practise the blurry-camera comparison until it sounds natural. Be ready to say the personas are unverified assumptions.
- **Speaker 2:** Be able to explain the k formula in one sentence, and explain why a fire neither sensor saw cannot be recovered.
- **Speaker 3:** Know the Kerala (47%) and Wayanad (113%) weekly errors, and that before 2012 cannot be tested directly. Know that in Kerala one national ratio has lower weekly error (33%).
- **Speaker 4:** Rehearse the demo at least three times. Keep slides 11–14 ready as the fallback. Open each news link once beforehand.
- **Speaker 5:** Be clear that India is built, and that global is only designed.

## Live demo steps (Speaker 4)

1. **Overview, Kerala:** read the sentence aloud: fires are detected most often from 12 Feb to 18 Mar. Explain the bars in one line, then point to "Why then?" and "How reliable is this?".
2. **Punjab & Haryana:** press the place. The peak moves to 22 Oct – 11 Nov, and the reason changes to stubble burning. Say detections fell, and we do not claim burning fell equally.
3. **Odisha, Which years stood out?:** the year opens on 2021 with the Similipal story. Read the line comparing it with the satellite record.
4. **See the problem on a map:** press **Play years** and pause just after 2012. The left map jumps, and the right map stays steady.
5. **Map:** scroll up and tap near Kochi to show that any area works, and that its reliability changes to Low.
6. **Detailed data:** open the tab, choose **Uttarakhand (approx.)**, press **Unusual weeks**, and hover over the week of 30 Apr in the 2024 row.
7. **Central India:** choose **Central India** and press **All fires detected** to show the autumn band growing in later years.

Direct links open a place in the overview, by adding `#kerala`, `#punjab_haryana`, `#odisha`, `#uttarakhand` or `#central` to the address. Adding `#details` opens Detailed data.

## Never say these

- A bare count of "fires". Say "detections" or "fires detected".
- "Burning in Punjab fell 92%." Say that detections fell by that much.
- "We predict" or "forecast". The calendar shows past patterns.
- "A live news feed." The stories are chosen and checked by hand, plus a Google News search link.
- "The news proves the cause." It shows what was reported at the time.
- "Central India's rise is proven to be crop burning." Our data fits the news reports, and field checks should confirm it.
- "Works globally." This release covers India.
- "Kerala 2022 was flagged", or "the Wayanad 2025 fire was detected as unusual". Neither was flagged.

- **3 minutes:** use slides 2, 4, 7, 9, 11, 12, 13, 15 and 18 only.

## Before you present

- [ ] Replace `[team name]` on slide 1, and the `[Speaker 2]` to `[Speaker 5]` names in the notes.
- [ ] Check the official challenge page. We could not open it, so the requirements came from your description.
- [ ] Call one forest official if you can. Even one conversation strengthens the user story.
- [ ] Ask two people with no background to use the overview for 30 seconds, then say what it tells them.
- [ ] Open each cited paper and news link, and confirm the claim. The Singh et al. 2025 paper was read through a secondary source only. Only some news stories were opened in full; the rest were checked through search results.
- [ ] Be able to explain any line of the code. Judges may ask, and your event's rules may require you to disclose AI assistance.
- [ ] Test the app on the presenting laptop, without Wi-Fi, using the steps in `README.md`. News links need internet.
