    Method

    MODIS sees mostly large fires, and VIIRS sees small ones too. We learned the gap for each place and month from 2012 to 2019, tested it on six years it never saw, and put all 25 years on one scale.

    Limits of the fire record

    • Counts detections, not fires

      Spots a satellite saw as unusually hot. Year-round heat such as coal fields is removed: 4% of the record.

    • Shows the past, not a forecast

      For fires burning now, see NASA FIRMS.

    • Misses some fires

      Satellites pass at set times and miss fires under cloud, smoke or trees.
