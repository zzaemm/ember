The problem AgniCal solves

Two NASA instruments watch for fires. MODIS, the older one, mostly sees larger fires. VIIRS, the newer one from 2012, is sharper and also sees small ones. It is like swapping a blurry camera for a sharp one: you suddenly count more, even if little has changed.

Good to know

What is a “detection”?

A spot that a satellite saw as unusually hot. It is usually a fire, but not always. We remove spots that stay hot all year, such as coal fields.

This shows the past

It is not a forecast or a live alert. For fires happening now, use NASA FIRMS or Forest Survey of India.

Satellites can miss fires

They pass only at set times and can miss small fires, or fires under cloud, thick smoke or trees. No detection does not mean no fire.
